Recurring design rules implemented in **Typography 1**:

- Condensed uppercase hero word with smaller setup and follow-through lines.
- Red, yellow, or blue rectangular strip chosen per caption phrase.
- Offset darker strip, hard extrusion, soft colored glow, and deep black shadow.
- Directional zoom entrance with a short defocus that resolves into sharp text.
- Progressive strip reveal and staggered top / hero / bottom text construction.
- Slow camera push while the phrase is held.
- Zoom and Gaussian-blur exit instead of a plain disappearance.
- Transparent output so the animation sits over the original video in Premiere or Resolve.

The reference videos remain external and are not copied into the product.
