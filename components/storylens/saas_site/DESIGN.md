# Hinglish Editor: professional product design

Replace the rejected cartoon landscape, tilted preview, purple surfaces and
oversized playful headings with a restrained, credible editing product.

Palette: white #ffffff, porcelain #f6f7f8, graphite #182027, slate #65717a,
line #e3e7ea, muted teal #38655f. Accent colours identify selected controls.
No decorative gradients, illustrated scenery or floating cards. A realistic
charcoal editing workspace and real licensed photography anchor the design.

Albert Sans is the locally hosted primary font. Weights 400–600, sentence case,
48–64px desktop display headlines, 36–42px mobile, 15–17px body text, 12–14px
workspace text. Use Barlow only for actual caption samples when appropriate.

Layout: centred introduction → wide editing workspace → compact workflow →
feature catalogue → straightforward pricing → FAQ. Clear alignment, generous
space, 6–10px corners and subtle depth on the product preview only. Avoid
decorative text treatments and repetitive marketing cards.

The photo is illustrative sample media, not evidence of a generated video.
Save its attribution and licence record. No invented testimonials or metrics.
Preserve public demo labels, launch status, account/payment/editing behavior.
Verify mobile layout, keyboard interaction, readable contrast and reduced motion.
