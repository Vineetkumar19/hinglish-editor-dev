Storylens layered Sound Design — 24 October 2026

IMPLEMENTED
Sound Design accepts an existing video/path or edited XML. Layered cinematic
sound is enabled by default; unchecking it retains restrained selection.
Existing speech transcription, prosody analysis, local Qwen story/emotion
planning and CLAP/library retrieval feed a new role-aware layer planner.
The planner selects complementary roles per measured event: anticipation,
travel, contact, detail and tail, with separate environmental bed/texture roles.
It may choose silence; density is a maximum budget, not a requirement to fill
every second. Up to three action layers and two ambient layers may overlap.
Music remains independently selected for the emotional arc.

Local Vision now sees 12 numbered frames per approximately six-second window.
Action intervals resolve from supplied frame indices. These support approximate
sustained Foley, not frame-exact contacts. Measured editor events and aligned
speech words retain their existing precise timestamp sources.

Mixing applies group gain compensation, speech-keyed effect ducking, the existing
music ducking/EQ and final limiter. Source-trimmed effects retain their requested
duration. Preview encoding now preserves stereo supplied mixes. Editable XML
keeps overlapping stereo effects on separate tracks, labeled with role/group.
Model decisions, timing uncertainty, scene observations and per-asset reasons
remain in the result's saved plan and report. No Epidemic private models/code,
AuroLA weights, MMAudio or FoleyCrafter runtime were installed in this work.
This is an independent local implementation, not recovered Epidemic AI.

VALIDATION
43 focused tests passed: tests.test_sound_layers, tests.test_sound_timing,
tests.test_sound. This includes actual WAV mixing and stereo MP4 encoding,
overlapping layer export to editable XML, candidate batching/index preservation,
fixed timing, source offsets, saved role metadata, and existing sound behavior.
Initial failing tests were observed for layer planning, ambient texture support,
stereo preview and malformed optional uncertainty. Batching/export integration
checks added afterward are supplementary checks, not claimed as prior TDD.

Final adversarial review reproduced two failures:
1. One ambient layer from one group plus two from another at the same time
   admitted three layers, exceeding the intended limit of two. This can crowd
   the mix. Fixed after explicit owner approval by counting the current group.
2. A model returning a list instead of a string event ID raised TypeError rather
   than skipping the malformed selection. Fixed after explicit owner approval
   by validating event ID, role and family types. Valid rows still survive.
Both regression tests failed before repair and pass afterward. Original and
post-repair receipts: output/sound-layer-validation/final-review*.json.

remain unverified until the real job completes. No native Premiere/CapCut
