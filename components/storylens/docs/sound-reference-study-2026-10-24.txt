OBSERVATIONS AND THEIR LIMITS
1. downloaded-video: the picture combines a creator montage and an editing
timeline. The early silhouette/text shots lead into notebook/pencil details at
about 2.5s, editing-app imagery around 4s, camera controls around 4.7–5.5s,
desk/face shots around 5.5–8.6s and an overhead desk view after about 8.75s.
The visible tracks use short clips and longer staggered beds. CLAP's mixed-audio
similarities favor early air movement/low hits, a swell around 4s and low decays
after 6s; camera-like transients are most prominent around 8s. These are sound
descriptors, not verified exact sources. Measured full-mix median falls from
roughly -16 dB in the opening to -42 dB at 8–10.5s, with isolated late activity.
Lesson: rapid tactile edits and low transitions can coexist with quieter space;
each new word or frame does not need the same bright three-clip gesture.

2. downloaded-video (1): automotive montage with an exposed multitrack layout.
Door/handle detail around 1.4–2s, departing car around 2.6–4.9s, rapid taillight/
wheel/exhaust/mirror details around 5.1–6.5s and badge/outro around 6.5–7.6s.
Many staggered short clips are visually distinct from longer audio textures.
Composite similarities favor pulsing/rough rhythmic material, a sweep around
5s and stronger low impacts at 6–8s. Full-mix median rises to about -11 dB at
5.3–7.6s, then decays. Exact engine/door sources and simultaneous layer counts
cannot be established from the flattened soundtrack or tiny track labels.
Lesson: rhythm, anticipation and punctuation develop with the sequence. This
does not justify inserting car sounds into a spoken joke that only names a van.

3. downloaded-video (2): cinematic SFX showcase. Rapid opening cuts give way to
city/silhouette shots, a car walk-by at about 6.55s, urban detail at 9.22s,
subway ceiling at 11.35s, city at 13.5s, then waveform/title cards from about
17.5s onward. The title cards are still part of the source and its soundtrack;
they must not be misrepresented as continuing action footage.
The full-mix median is about -34.5 dB at 3.8–6.55s versus -13.9 dB at 20.9–25s.
That is relative quiet, not proven absolute silence or an isolated music mute.
Composite similarities shift toward sustained strings/score-like textures around
11–19s, low-impact/pulse clusters around 20–24s and 26–28s, then decay. Whether
a tonal texture is music or designed SFX is uncertain without original stems.
Lesson: subtle presence, sustained tonal space and large impacts have different
jobs. Constant density would remove the contrast this sample demonstrates.

These three are cinematic/editor-timeline/automotive references. They do not
independently prove a catalogue of comic stings. Earlier owner E7/e2/e5 examples
and the owner's direct funny/serious direction are preserved for that scope.

WHY STORYLENS SOUNDED REPETITIVE
The actual Zomato plan has 76 effect clips, of which 55 are in poke/cute families,
and 21 groups repeat contact/detail/tail roles. Its only music section reuses
Sneaky Snitch. SPEECH_MOTIFS expands names into fixed acoustic tuples. Therefore
more filenames and more clips do not necessarily produce different composition.
The music catalogue is also small. This study does not claim those engine
constraints have been redesigned or the output is now creatively fixed.
