4. The direct Storylens differences to address

This is a reconstruction recommendation, not recovered proprietary AI:
- Represent a graphic's small sub-events and short source excerpts, so a 2s
  animated sequence can legitimately hold several staggered accents.
- Preserve baseline word accents AND let the creative motif planner augment
  speech/graphic events. Passing only physical scene evidence to the second
  planner cannot construct motion-based layers for ordinary talking footage.
- Keep physical Foley evidence conservative, while explicitly permitting
  motivated nonliteral graphic/comedy sounds. These are different purposes.
- Compare actual motif coverage and sound durations against the owner's final
  edit, rather than counting a continuous bed as newly triggered detail.
- Evaluate group attenuation: current Storylens subtracts 10*log10(group size)
  and 4dB speech ducking. Three ingredients therefore lose about 8.77dB before
  other safety caps. Independent role envelopes and short anticipations/tails
  can preserve clarity without making every extra layer equally weak.
None of these recommendations requires copying Epidemic's catalogue or putting
five arbitrary effects everywhere. Denser relevant output needs more motivated
sub-events and suitable short excerpts, not wildlife under indoor dialogue.
