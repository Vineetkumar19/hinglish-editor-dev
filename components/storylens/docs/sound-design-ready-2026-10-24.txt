Implemented behavior
- Per-video acoustic identity and independently authored layer intents replace
  the default repeated speech motif ensembles. Legacy saved plans remain.
- Candidate retrieval considers up to 24 relevant files, then gives the model
  a bounded, varied six-file slate. Exact duplicate bytes are deduplicated.
  Repeated sources require an explicit recurring motif and narrative reason.
  The model can still reject every candidate; no random replacement is forced.
- New line-anchored spotting sections support music, effects_only and
  voice_only. They use actual supplied line IDs and timing, cover lines without
  overlaps, and allow intentional dry passages without muting the voice.
  Old saved music plans retain their previous bed behavior.
- Music fades/filter tails do not leak into explicit no-music intervals.
  Effects are excluded from voice-only anchors, and pre-roll/tails are trimmed
  without moving the source event relative to the timeline.
- Optional per-layer filters, speech-frequency dip, stereo pan, fades and
  speech ducking are bounded typed settings with an explanation. They are
  rendered into editor stems and saved with processing receipts. No arbitrary
  plugin code, shell expressions or unsupported processor is executed.
- Candidate alternatives and measured source-offset synchronization metadata
  remain with each cue. Energy peaks are not labeled verified semantic contacts.
- Music off/on keeps immutable saved track/excerpt choices. Invalid saved
  processing is rejected before replacing the prior preview or teaching taste.
- Shared errors explain the next step and retain Technical details. QA accounts
  for intentionally authored speech ducking while still rejecting silent output.
- Mastered stems keep the common gain/limiter envelope and existing native
  editor handoff paths. Approved captions and other feature designs are untouched.
