Pocket Tracker brings bank SMS, selected Gmail alerts and supported payment notifications together on your Android phone.

What the app does

  • Shows debit and credit events as bank SMS arrive.
  • Receives optional payment alerts from Paytm, Slice and BHIM.
  • Shows payment notes when they are included in source alerts.
  • Matches transaction evidence to reduce double counting.
  • Creates monthly PDFs locally, with missing coverage and uncertain alerts clearly marked.
  • Offers recurring-payment plans, a monthly budget, and an optional encrypted same-Wi-Fi laptop dashboard.

Designed for personal use

This is an independently developed personal-use app, not a public banking service. It does not initiate payments, ask for banking passwords or access another app’s private payment history.

How Gmail is used

With your consent, the Android app connects directly to Google’s Gmail API using read-only mailbox permission. The finance importer fetches messages matching the configured search rule. A separate automatic incoming-email categorizer organizes local warning signs, promotions and Gmail Spam. Extracting amounts, matching evidence and creating reports take place on the phone.

Understand the limits

Entries depend on alerts being delivered. Notes kept only inside a payment app are not accessible. Payment-app alerts without a strong bank-evidence match stay separate from confirmed totals. Android may delay background work, and Google may require renewed authorization.

Read the privacy policy and terms of use before granting access.
