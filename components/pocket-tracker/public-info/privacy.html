Privacy policy

This policy describes Pocket Tracker, an Android app for personal financial-alert tracking, and this static information website.

Data accessed and purpose

  • SMS: With Android permission, the app reads incoming SMS and imports recent inbox history. It retains financial, suspicious and promotional messages used for tracking and local categorization. Ordinary personal and OTP messages are normally excluded by local rules; rules are not infallible.
  • Gmail: With Google authorization, the app requests gmail.readonly. The permission is mailbox-wide; the finance importer fetches messages matching the configured selection rule. Automatic Safety categorization also reads recent incoming messages, including Gmail Spam, and retains only financial alerts or messages assigned a local safety/promotional category. Ordinary message bodies examined for classification are discarded; processed message identifiers may be retained to avoid repeated downloads. It uses sender, subject, message content, timestamps and identifiers to extract and match financial alerts. It cannot send, delete or modify email.
  • Payment notifications: Optional Android notification access is broad at the system level. The app filters notifications by package before reading content and processes financial alerts only from Paytm, Slice and BHIM. It uses available text, notes, times and identifiers. It does not click notification actions.

Local storage and security

Retained message sender and body content are encrypted using an Android Keystore key. Message identifiers, timestamps, source labels and synchronization metadata are held in app-private storage. Google Play services manages authorization tokens. Generated PDFs are private app files, but are not additionally encrypted; opening or sharing a PDF gives the chosen viewer access to that file. Android automatic backup is disabled for the app.

Sharing and external services

Pocket Tracker has no analytics, advertising, remote AI or developer-operated message-processing server. Message content is not uploaded to this website or the developer. The phone communicates with Google to authorize and read Gmail data for the purposes above. An optional paired laptop can request financial records, retained message details, reports, and planning settings over an authenticated TLS connection on the same local network. The laptop companion pins the phone certificate and keeps a private pairing credential; dashboard data is held in memory, not uploaded to a public site. PDFs deliberately downloaded on the laptop remain there until you delete them. Google processes those requests under its own policies. Data you choose to share or export is handled by the receiving application or recipient.

Pocket Tracker’s use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. Google user data is not sold, used for advertising, used to train generalized AI models, or transferred to data brokers.

Retention and control

The financial history import window is 100 days. Automatic incoming-email categorization begins with the preceding day and continues from its saved checkpoint. Imported records and reports remain locally until the app’s data is cleared or the app is uninstalled; there is no automatic rolling deletion of previously imported records. You can revoke Google access in your Google Account, and revoke SMS or notification access in Android settings. Revoking permission stops future access but does not delete already stored records. Clearing app data or uninstalling removes local records and the local key; exported copies must be deleted separately.

Website

This static site has no forms, accounts, analytics scripts or application-data uploads. GitHub Pages serves the site and may process ordinary request metadata under GitHub’s privacy statement.

Support and changes

Contact the developer through the support address shown on the Google consent configuration. This page will be updated if the app’s data practices change.
