# Gaming Desk

Open Launch Gaming Desk.vbs or http://127.0.0.1:8880/.

Independent gaming inbox for new games, announcements, patches, trailers, community reactions and memes. Latest gaming discovers broadly; a specific game/event focuses collection. Keep/Skip, save accessible images, export selected sources, and choose domains, lookback, dates and research timing under Settings. Original URLs, authors and publication evidence stay with each candidate. Feed results are candidates, not verified facts or guaranteed video monetization. Reels/posts may remain source links; this tool does not bypass login or download every video.

Owner requested the same Cricket Desk workflow. Initial feeds and GPT are ON: feeds every 30 minutes; GPT every 30 minutes from 18:00 until 00:00 IST (exclusive), maximum 12 attempts/day. They have separate controls and usage from Cricket Desk. Change or pause them independently in the UI. Both desks enabled can consume separate GPT research allowances. GPT uses official ChatGPT-authenticated Codex CLI live web search, not Chrome automation or local reasoning models. No API key is installed. No publishing occurs.

All gaming data/media live under this folder, separate from Cricket Desk. Public source availability and rate limits determine collection coverage. Inspect validation-result.json for actual run evidence; do not infer end-to-end GPT acceptance from installation alone.
